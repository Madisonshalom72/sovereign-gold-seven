The Five Finals — sofit

Five letters wear a second, longer shape when they end a word in the square Hebrew script: ך → כ (Kaf) · ם → מ (Mem) · ן → נ (Nun) · ף → פ (Pe) · ץ → צ (Sade). It is the same letter with the same sound — and in Paleo Hebrew there are no finals at all: one letter, one shape, wherever it stands. That is why every word on the tablets page is written with the single ancient forms you are learning here.
