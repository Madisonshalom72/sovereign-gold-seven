House Rules

  1. Real names or attested handles. Attend under the name the system knows you by, or the handle you publicly stand behind. No anonymous masks in the sovereign's hall.
  2. No financial advice is given or taken in this hall. Nothing said here is a recommendation to buy, sell, hold, or acquire anything — the Hall hosts community and education, never advice.
  3. No coin business in the hall. No offers, no sales, no prices negotiated, no promises made.
  4. Sessions happen on the announced schedule. The Court sits when the WIRE says it sits; announcements are made through the ecosystem's channels, never by rumor in the room.
  5. The signature rule. "Your signature, or it doesn't count" applies to the Hall itself: nothing said, agreed, or "decided" in a Hall session is official until it is recorded in the system's own records. The Hall is where things are discussed; the record is where things are.
  6. The red light is announced. Open sessions are recorded for the public record show — announced before the red light, every time. Closed Chamber work is never recorded.

Announced. Never rumored.

Sessions are announced on SG7 WIRE and the ecosystem pages — with the date, the time, and the host named in advance. A cancelled session is announced as cancelled through the same channels that announced it. The Chamber · The Inner Room

The chamber is not on this page.

Behind the Court sits the Chamber: the working room of the system's appointed people — the Founding Principals, the holders of the Seven Seats, and the appointed role-holders of the Role Reserve — for governance discussion, design review, and coordination.

Entry is by invitation only, and the invitation is the key. The Chamber's door is never published — not on this page, not in any post, not in any announcement. Its room is named fresh for each sitting and the name is retired when the sitting ends, so a door that escapes opens nothing. If you are meant to be in the Chamber, you will be handed the way in by name; if you found a way in somewhere else, it is not the Chamber.

Today, pre-issuance, the Chamber runs on that invitation discipline alone — and this page says so plainly rather than pretending a check exists that doesn't. The Chamber activates in full at issuance: when the legal gate passes and the seats and roles are actually filled, entry binds to the system's attestation — the right to be in the room derives from an attendee's attested standing, checked against the same records the rest of the system keeps.

Chamber sittings are never recorded, and they mint no commitments: like everything in this hall, nothing discussed there is official until it is recorded in the system's own records. Being invited to the Chamber confers no allocation, no seat, no role, and no claim.
