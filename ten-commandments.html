The Tablets

Written in the ancient hand.

The Paleo line is the consonantal Hebrew of Exodus 20 (Miqra according to the Masorah), mapped letter by letter into the Phoenician-letter script Israel wrote with in the days of the kings. The King James text stands beneath each tablet.

BEFORE THE LAW — THE SPEAKER NAMES HIMSELF

𐤀𐤍𐤊𐤉 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 𐤀𐤋𐤄𐤉𐤊 𐤀𐤔𐤓 𐤄𐤅𐤑𐤀𐤕𐤉𐤊 𐤌𐤀𐤓𐤑 𐤌𐤑𐤓𐤉𐤌 𐤌𐤁𐤉𐤕 𐤏𐤁𐤃𐤉𐤌

I am the LORD thy God, which have brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.

EXODUS 20:2 · KING JAMES VERSION
I

No other gods

𐤋𐤀 𐤉𐤄𐤉𐤄 𐤋𐤊 𐤀𐤋𐤄𐤉𐤌 𐤀𐤇𐤓𐤉𐤌 𐤏𐤋 𐤐𐤍𐤉

Thou shalt have no other gods before me.

EXODUS 20:3 · KING JAMES VERSION
II

No graven image

𐤋𐤀 𐤕𐤏𐤔𐤄 𐤋𐤊 𐤐𐤎𐤋 𐤅𐤊𐤋 𐤕𐤌𐤅𐤍𐤄 𐤀𐤔𐤓 𐤁𐤔𐤌𐤉𐤌 𐤌𐤌𐤏𐤋 𐤅𐤀𐤔𐤓 𐤁𐤀𐤓𐤑 𐤌𐤕𐤇𐤕 𐤅𐤀𐤔𐤓 𐤁𐤌𐤉𐤌 𐤌𐤕𐤇𐤕 𐤋𐤀𐤓𐤑

𐤋𐤀 𐤕𐤔𐤕𐤇𐤅𐤄 𐤋𐤄𐤌 𐤅𐤋𐤀 𐤕𐤏𐤁𐤃𐤌 𐤊𐤉 𐤀𐤍𐤊𐤉 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 𐤀𐤋𐤄𐤉𐤊 𐤀𐤋 𐤒𐤍𐤀 𐤐𐤒𐤃 𐤏𐤅𐤍 𐤀𐤁𐤕 𐤏𐤋 𐤁𐤍𐤉𐤌 𐤏𐤋 𐤔𐤋𐤔𐤉𐤌 𐤅𐤏𐤋 𐤓𐤁𐤏𐤉𐤌 𐤋𐤔𐤍𐤀𐤉

𐤅𐤏𐤔𐤄 𐤇𐤎𐤃 𐤋𐤀𐤋𐤐𐤉𐤌 𐤋𐤀𐤄𐤁𐤉 𐤅𐤋𐤔𐤌𐤓𐤉 𐤌𐤑𐤅𐤕𐤉

Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth: Thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them: for I the LORD thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me; And shewing mercy unto thousands of them that love me, and keep my commandments.

EXODUS 20:4–6 · KING JAMES VERSION
III

The Name, not in vain

𐤋𐤀 𐤕𐤔𐤀 𐤀𐤕 𐤔𐤌 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 𐤀𐤋𐤄𐤉𐤊 𐤋𐤔𐤅𐤀 𐤊𐤉 𐤋𐤀 𐤉𐤍𐤒𐤄 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 𐤀𐤕 𐤀𐤔𐤓 𐤉𐤔𐤀 𐤀𐤕 𐤔𐤌𐤅 𐤋𐤔𐤅𐤀

Thou shalt not take the name of the LORD thy God in vain; for the LORD will not hold him guiltless that taketh his name in vain.

EXODUS 20:7 · KING JAMES VERSION
IV

Remember the Sabbath

𐤆𐤊𐤅𐤓 𐤀𐤕 𐤉𐤅𐤌 𐤄𐤔𐤁𐤕 𐤋𐤒𐤃𐤔𐤅

𐤔𐤔𐤕 𐤉𐤌𐤉𐤌 𐤕𐤏𐤁𐤃 𐤅𐤏𐤔𐤉𐤕 𐤊𐤋 𐤌𐤋𐤀𐤊𐤕𐤊

𐤅𐤉𐤅𐤌 𐤄𐤔𐤁𐤉𐤏𐤉 𐤔𐤁𐤕 𐤋𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 𐤀𐤋𐤄𐤉𐤊 𐤋𐤀 𐤕𐤏𐤔𐤄 𐤊𐤋 𐤌𐤋𐤀𐤊𐤄 𐤀𐤕𐤄 𐤅𐤁𐤍𐤊 𐤅𐤁𐤕𐤊 𐤏𐤁𐤃𐤊 𐤅𐤀𐤌𐤕𐤊 𐤅𐤁𐤄𐤌𐤕𐤊 𐤅𐤂𐤓𐤊 𐤀𐤔𐤓 𐤁𐤔𐤏𐤓𐤉𐤊

𐤊𐤉 𐤔𐤔𐤕 𐤉𐤌𐤉𐤌 𐤏𐤔𐤄 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 𐤀𐤕 𐤄𐤔𐤌𐤉𐤌 𐤅𐤀𐤕 𐤄𐤀𐤓𐤑 𐤀𐤕 𐤄𐤉𐤌 𐤅𐤀𐤕 𐤊𐤋 𐤀𐤔𐤓 𐤁𐤌 𐤅𐤉𐤍𐤇 𐤁𐤉𐤅𐤌 𐤄𐤔𐤁𐤉𐤏𐤉 𐤏𐤋 𐤊𐤍 𐤁𐤓𐤊 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 𐤀𐤕 𐤉𐤅𐤌 𐤄𐤔𐤁𐤕 𐤅𐤉𐤒𐤃𐤔𐤄𐤅

Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy. Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work: But the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates: For in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is, and rested the seventh day: wherefore the LORD blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it.

EXODUS 20:8–11 · KING JAMES VERSION
V

Honour thy father and thy mother

𐤊𐤁𐤃 𐤀𐤕 𐤀𐤁𐤉𐤊 𐤅𐤀𐤕 𐤀𐤌𐤊 𐤋𐤌𐤏𐤍 𐤉𐤀𐤓𐤊𐤅𐤍 𐤉𐤌𐤉𐤊 𐤏𐤋 𐤄𐤀𐤃𐤌𐤄 𐤀𐤔𐤓 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 𐤀𐤋𐤄𐤉𐤊 𐤍𐤕𐤍 𐤋𐤊

Honour thy father and thy mother: that thy days may be long upon the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.

EXODUS 20:12 · KING JAMES VERSION
VI

Thou shalt not kill

𐤋𐤀 𐤕𐤓𐤑𐤇

Thou shalt not kill.

EXODUS 20:13 · KING JAMES VERSION
VII

No adultery

𐤋𐤀 𐤕𐤍𐤀𐤐

Thou shalt not commit adultery.

EXODUS 20:14 · KING JAMES VERSION
VIII

Thou shalt not steal

𐤋𐤀 𐤕𐤂𐤍𐤁

Thou shalt not steal.

EXODUS 20:15 · KING JAMES VERSION
IX

No false witness

𐤋𐤀 𐤕𐤏𐤍𐤄 𐤁𐤓𐤏𐤊 𐤏𐤃 𐤔𐤒𐤓

Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour.

EXODUS 20:16 · KING JAMES VERSION
X

Thou shalt not covet

𐤋𐤀 𐤕𐤇𐤌𐤃 𐤁𐤉𐤕 𐤓𐤏𐤊 𐤋𐤀 𐤕𐤇𐤌𐤃 𐤀𐤔𐤕 𐤓𐤏𐤊 𐤅𐤏𐤁𐤃𐤅 𐤅𐤀𐤌𐤕𐤅 𐤅𐤔𐤅𐤓𐤅 𐤅𐤇𐤌𐤓𐤅 𐤅𐤊𐤋 𐤀𐤔𐤓 𐤋𐤓𐤏𐤊

Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's house, thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's wife, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any thing that is thy neighbour's.

EXODUS 20:17 · KING JAMES VERSION

A NOTE ON THE NUMBERING — IN THE HEBREW TEXT, COMMANDMENTS VI–IX STAND TOGETHER IN A SINGLE VERSE (EXODUS 20:13); THEY ARE SET ON SEPARATE TABLETS HERE, AS THE KING JAMES NUMBERS THEM (20:13–16). THE WORDS ARE THE SAME; ONLY THE VERSE DIVISION DIFFERS.
