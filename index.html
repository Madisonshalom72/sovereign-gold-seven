01 · The Thesis

DOGE IS ATTENTION.
SG7 IS AUTHORIZED MONEY.

In September 2026, Amazon blocked Meta's Muse shopping agent from its marketplace — the agent could not reliably prove who it was acting for, or what that person had actually approved. The industry's verdict came in four words: access is not authority. The same month, FinCEN and federal banking regulators recognized verifiable credentials for customer identity — and in October, Proof put x401, signed agent authorization, into production. SG7 takes that pattern and makes it monetary design.

SIGNED

Every agent spend carries the owner's signature over the exact terms — amount, recipient, purpose. Change any field and the signature no longer validates.

VERIFIED

Every principal is identity-verified — the human behind the agent, and the agent behind the payment. No anonymous money movement, by design.

NANO

Settlement sized for the machine economy — single claims worth fractions of a cent, paid per claim, instantly, without accounts or card minimums.

04 · How It Works

The signature is the security.

The x401 pattern, in plain English. The owner sets the rules once; the agent can only ever act inside them — and anyone can check.

STEP 01

REQUEST

The agent presents a payment request — the exact amount, recipient, and purpose — together with the identity of the human it acts for.

STEP 02

APPROVE

The owner approves a mandate once, on their own device: what the agent may buy, from whom, up to what limit, until when.

STEP 03

SIGN

The wallet signs the exact terms. Amount, recipient, purpose, limits — the signature covers every field of the deal.

STEP 04

VERIFY

The receiving side verifies the signature against the mandate. Anything altered fails verification — and nothing settles.

CHANGE THE DEAL, BREAK THE SIGNATURE.
WORKED EXAMPLE — A contractor's Alice receptionist needs a verified property measurement to quote a job. The data service quotes $0.006 for the claim. The receptionist's mandate — signed by the contractor — allows data purchases up to $0.50 per day from approved services. The claim is inside the mandate, identity verifies on both ends, six-tenths of a cent settles instantly, and the quote goes out. Total human involvement: one signature, given days earlier.

08 · The Road to Value

A sequence, not a promise.

No dates are attached to any phase. A roadmap with dates would be a promise; this is an order of operations — and the legal gate sits inside the foundation, before anything else can move.

PHASE ONE

FOUNDATION WE ARE HERE

This paper. This site. Name, brand, and trade dress development; token parameters and the mandate specification move toward design lock. The ecosystem keeps operating and growing without the coin.

LEGAL REVIEW GATE

Independent legal review of the entire design — securities law, money transmission, tax, and consumer protection in every jurisdiction the project would touch — must be completed, and its conclusions satisfied, before any issuance, sale, or distribution is even scheduled. If the review says the design cannot proceed as drawn, the design changes or the project stops. There is no issuance before this gate. There are no exceptions to this gate.

PHASE TWO

THE SWARM LAUNCHES

Non-transferable test assets on a test network; mandate and settlement flows exercised against the live ecosystem in sandbox form. And the nanobot gate opens in earnest — acquire the swarm, access the standard.

PHASE THREE

SETTLEMENT GOES LIVE

Only after the gate: a separate written issuance decision, with final parameters, disclosures, and compliance measures published in advance. SG7 settlement begins across the ecosystem's own products first — where the utility already lives.

PHASE FOUR

THE TREASURY BUILDS

The gold-referenced treasury policy executes from ecosystem revenue on its published schedule — disclosed custodian, regular reporting — while the burn schedule runs alongside it, drawing supply toward the Seven Floor.

PHASE FIVE

THE STANDARD WIDENS

More products, services, and partners honor the Seven Standard — agent tiers, station layers, training, local services. The ecosystem grows the coin's usefulness. That is the only growth this design counts on.
